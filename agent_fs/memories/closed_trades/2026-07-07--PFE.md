## Thesis
Evaluated PFE for a one-week swing trade. The stock shows oversold technical conditions (RSI 30, Stochastics 15/14, Williams %R -85) but is in a strong, volume-confirmed downtrend (ADX 40, CMF -0.355, OBV falling all timeframes, price below all SMAs). Oversold in a strong downtrend is typically a continuation signal, not a reversal.

## Key Metrics
- RSI(14): 30.0 (oversold threshold)
- ADX(14): 40.0 (strong trend)
- CMF(20): -0.355 (heavy distribution)
- Forward P/E: 8.5 (cheap)
- Dividend Yield: 7.25%
- 1-month vs SPY: -7.77% underperformance
- Sharpe (1Y): 0.01
- Analyst consensus: Buy, mean target $29.15 (+21.3%)

## Bull Case
- Oversold cluster could trigger mean-reversion bounce to SMA20 (~$24.90)
- Forward P/E 8.5 is extremely cheap
- 7.25% dividend yield provides floor
- Low beta (0.31) limits downside
- 27 analysts at Buy with 21% upside

## Bear Case
- Strong downtrend with institutional distribution
- Price below all major SMAs
- Oversold in strong trend = continuation, not reversal
- Earnings -10.1%, revenue decelerating
- Relative strength falling sharply vs SPY
- Sharpe near zero — no risk compensation

## Decision
Rejected for 1-week hold. Risk/reward unfavorable. The oversold bounce thesis is plausible but the weight of trend, volume, and relative strength evidence is against it. Better suited for long-term value investors, not short-term swing traders.

## Lessons
- When ADX > 35 and CMF is deeply negative, oversold signals are continuation signals, not reversal signals
- Always check volume confirmation (OBV, CMF) alongside momentum oscillators
- Relative strength vs SPY provides crucial context — a stock can look "cheap" on oscillators but still be in a relative downtrend
- Forward P/E of 8.5 is a long-term value signal, not a short-term timing signal
- **Follow-up (2026-07-07):** PFE fell from $24.02 to $24.01 over the month — the rejection was correct. The oversold-in-downtrend continuation thesis played out. ADX > 35 + negative CMF remains a reliable fade signal for oversold bounces.
