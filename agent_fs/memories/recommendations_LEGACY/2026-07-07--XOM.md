## Lessons
- **What worked:** The structural filter (above SMA200 + positive CMF) correctly differentiated this from the PFE fade. Oversold signals in a bull-trend context are reliable mean-reversion setups.
- **What to improve:** The target should be dynamic — SMA20 can compress downward, so a fixed-price target at entry may overstate potential gains. A better approach: target "convergence with SMA20" as a dynamic level, not a fixed price.
- **Key signal validated:** RSIs below 30 with positive CMF and price above SMA200 are high-probability bounce setups. The ADX being elevated (33.5→49.2) did NOT prevent the bounce; it just indicated the move was powerful in both directions.
- **Risk management:** The stop at $133.20 was never threatened. The trade had room to breathe below the 200 SMA.
