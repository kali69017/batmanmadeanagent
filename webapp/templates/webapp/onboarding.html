What Reflex does

Reflex is an automated research tool. Every trading day, it screens a watchlist of stocks, analyzes the strongest candidates, and surfaces picks with a suggested buy range, target, and exit point.

It doesn't place trades for you. You decide what to act on.

2

What Confidence means

Each pick shows a Confidence badge: High, Medium, or Low. This reflects how strong the signal looked when it was generated, not a guarantee of outcome.

High confidence picks have historically performed better on average, but every pick carries risk. Check the Portfolio tab for actual win rate and average return, not just today's badges.

3

Not financial advice

Reflex provides research and analysis, not personalized investment advice. Nothing in this app is a recommendation to buy or sell any security.

Markets carry risk, including loss of principal. Past performance shown in this app does not predict future results. You are responsible for your own investment decisions, and should consult a licensed financial advisor before acting on anything here.
